Channel Hub

A personal tool for reading the analytics of YouTube channels its owner runs.

What it does

Channel Hub runs on its owner's own computer. With read-only access, it downloads statistics for the owner's YouTube channels from YouTube API Services and turns them into a private dashboard and reports: which videos perform best, where viewers stop watching, how people find the videos, and ideas for what to make next.

For channels where the owner turns on publishing, it can also upload the owner's finished videos as private drafts and schedule them to go public, but only after the owner approves each one.

Who it is for

Only the owner of the channels it connects to. It is not a public service and it has no user accounts. It never deletes videos, posts comments, or makes anything public without the owner's approval.

Your data

Read the privacy policy for what Channel Hub accesses and how it is stored. Channel Hub uses YouTube API Services; by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to data Google processes.

Revoke Channel Hub's access at any time from your Google Account third-party connections.
