Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Channel Hub is a personal tool that runs on its owner's computer and is used only by the owner of the YouTube channels it connects to. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

Channel Hub uses YouTube API Services. By using Channel Hub you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

Channel Hub asks Google for two read-only permissions and nothing else:

  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): the connected channel's name, handle and public counts, its list of uploaded videos, and each video's title, duration, publish date, privacy status and public view, like and comment counts.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): channel and video analytics such as views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, the YouTube searches that led to the videos, thumbnail impressions and click-through rate, and subscribers gained and lost.

It cannot upload, edit, delete or post anything, and it does not read comments, messages or personal profile data.

How it is used

The data is used only to build a dashboard and written reports for the channel owner, to help plan and improve videos. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on the owner's computer: a local database, report files and the Google sign-in token, in a folder only the owner's computer account can read. Channel Hub has no server, no cloud copy, and no analytics, cookies or trackers. The downloaded data is kept while the channel stays connected, and it is refreshed by each sync.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke Channel Hub's access at any time from the third-party connections page of your Google Account (also reachable at Google security settings). Every sync uses the saved permission, so syncing stops as soon as access is revoked. Stored data can be deleted by removing Channel Hub's data folder, and any deletion request is completed within 7 days.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at github.com/sha-shank-03/channel-hub-site/issues.
